1. Introduction

Welcome to Ludo Classic ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users ("user," "you," or "your").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download, install, play, or interact with our mobile game application Ludo Classic (the "Game"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using our Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways depending on how you interact with our Game.

a) Information Collected Automatically

When you play Ludo Classic, our app and third-party partners may automatically collect certain technical information from your mobile device, including:

  • Device Identification & Identifiers: Unique Device Identifier (IDFA/GAID), IP address, and advertising ID.
  • Device Information: Hardware model, operating system version, system language, battery level, and network status.
  • Gameplay Data: Game scores, dice roll statistics, win/loss history, levels achieved, and local game preferences.
  • Log & Diagnostics Data: Crash reports, system activity, performance logs, and timestamp data.

b) Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact our support team via email or form, we may collect your name, email address, and any contents of your message to assist you.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes to provide and improve your gaming experience:

  • To operate, maintain, and update the Game functions (pass & play, local vs computer mode, online room setups).
  • To save your local game progress and user settings across sessions.
  • To deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements (where permitted).
  • To analyze game performance, detect bugs, and resolve technical issues.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and ensure fair play among all players.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Ludo Classic is designed to be a fun, family-friendly board game for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU jurisdictions).

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, we will promptly take steps to delete such information from our records. Parents or guardians who believe their child has submitted personal data may contact us to request deletion.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

Depending on your region (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA/CPRA in California), you have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request details about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion / Eradication: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information and game history.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based advertising directly from your device settings (e.g., Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete Advertising ID on Android, or Settings > Privacy > Tracking on iOS).

Request Data Deletion: To request deletion of any stored data associated with your gameplay, please send an email to support@ludogame.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Ludo Classic".
