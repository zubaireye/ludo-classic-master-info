Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Application: Ludo Classic Master

Developer: Aim Tech Park / Zubair Bin Tarque

Thank you for playing Ludo Classic Master. We are committed to respecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data when using our Android mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Ludo Classic Master is primarily designed as an offline mobile board game. We collect minimal data necessary for providing advertising and game services:

  • Local Game Data: Your game progress, coins, level, achievements, and board themes are stored 100% locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Room Database). We do not transmit or store your game save files on external private servers without your action.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics (AdMob & Firebase): Our application uses Google AdMob for displaying non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and performance diagnostics to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

⚠️ Virtual Currency & Gaming Policy Disclaimer: Ludo Classic Master uses virtual coins and points strictly for local game progression, board theme customization, and single-player/local gameplay. No real money, cash prizes, real-world rewards, or cash withdrawals are supported or offered in this game.

2. Use of Information

The minimal data collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To enable offline gameplay, saving of high scores, and theme customization.
  • To serve relevant advertisements via Google AdMob SDK.
  • To allow users to optionally export or backup local database progress to their personal Google Drive storage.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app incorporates the following trusted third-party SDK services compliant with Google Play Policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Analytics & App Check Privacy

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Ludo Classic Master is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad networks, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

5. Data Security

Your local game data is kept securely on your device's internal application storage sandbox. Any network communication handled by third-party ad networks uses standard HTTPS encryption protocols.

6. Your Data Rights & Choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID. You can also clear all game data by clearing app storage under Android settings.
